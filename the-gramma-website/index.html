Why The Gramma

Handcrafted. Completely yours.

A γράμμα is a personal letter, written for one person. That is how we work. We do not fill in templates. We begin with a blank page, your story and a pen.

Drawn, not downloaded

Every invitation is illustrated, lettered and animated for a single celebration. There is no shelf of designs to choose from.

Personal to the last detail

Your names, your palette, your language, your favourite line of a song. We ask questions until it sounds like you.

Opened, never scrolled past

How It Works
